ResQ – User Roles and Permissions

AI-Powered Emergency Assistance & Community Response Platform

1. Roles

RoleDescriptionMain Responsibilities
CitizenA community member who reports problems such as road damage, flooding or electrical hazards.Submit incidents with photos and location, track their own reports, receive notifications.
Administrator / DispatcherThe person who manages the platform and coordinates responses.Review incidents and AI analysis, approve or reject, set priority, assign response teams, manage categories, teams and users, view dashboard, reports and audit logs.
Response Team MemberA member of a department team (e.g. Road Maintenance) that fixes incidents.View assigned incidents, acknowledge, update progress, upload resolution photos, mark completed.
